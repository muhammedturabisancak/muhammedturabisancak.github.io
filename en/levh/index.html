APP · iOS AND ANDROID

Levh

Prayer time, under a calm sky. Prayer times, qibla, dhikr, missed-prayer tracking and religious days; no ads, and no internet needed.

Coming soonApp Store Coming soonGoogle Play

01 · Today

The next prayer, with its sky.

The sky behind the countdown follows the time of day: moon and stars at night, sun by day, sunset after Asr. Times are calculated on your phone.

02 · Qibla

The qibla, always in the tab bar.

A ring in the tab bar shows how close you are to the qibla; tap it for a full-screen compass. Magnetic declination is corrected.

03 · Dhikr

A dhikr screen without distractions.

Arabic text with transliteration and meaning. Reach your goal and it moves on to the next one; the screen stays awake.

04 · Missed prayers

Make up missed prayers, day by day.

Enter what you owe once, then tick off as you pray. See what's left, your milestones and an estimated finish date.

05 · Religious days

Holy nights and Eids, ready in advance.

Days left, that evening's adhan time, the Eid prayer time in your provincial centre and a greeting card to share.

Privacy

Your time stays on your phone.

No ads

No ads, sponsored content or tracking on any screen.

No data collected

Your location is used only on your phone to calculate prayer times; it is never sent anywhere.

Works offline

Prayer times, qibla and content all work offline.
