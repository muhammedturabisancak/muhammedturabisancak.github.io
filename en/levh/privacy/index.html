Levh

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026
TR EN

01

In short

Levh collects none of your personal data and sends nothing to us or anyone else. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not use an internet connection.

02

Location

If you allow it, your approximate location is used on your phone only to find the nearest district and calculate prayer times. It is never sent anywhere; the only thing stored is the name of the province and district you chose. If you'd rather not grant permission, you can pick your province and district from a list.

03

What stays on your phone

Your theme, language and text size preferences; your chosen location; your notification preferences; the verses, hadiths and names you save; your dhikr counts and goals; and your missed prayer and fasting records. All of it stays on your phone only and is deleted when you delete the app.

04

Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled on your phone; there is no notification server. On Android, system permissions are used to set reminders again after your phone restarts and to show them exactly on time. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.

05

Sharing

When you share a piece of content or a greeting card, your phone's share sheet opens. You choose what is shared and with whom; Levh plays no part in it.

06

Children

Levh collects no personal data from anyone, so it collects none from children either.

07

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes. If you have any questions, write to muhammedturabisancak@gmail.com.
