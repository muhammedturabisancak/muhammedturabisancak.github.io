İlim Kervanı

A journey from city to city. 6 cities and 120 levels from Mecca to Istanbul. Collect stars, earn badges.

Coming soonApp Store Coming soonGoogle Play

01 · City road

One road from Mecca to Istanbul.

Cities are stops on the journey; every level mixes questions from the Qur'an, Seerah, Ethics, Fiqh, Hadith and Islamic History. A level has 10 questions; pass with at least 6 correct and earn up to 3 stars. Finish a city to earn its seal.

02 · Play and lifelines

Every answer has a source.

4 options, 20 seconds per question. After you answer, you see a short explanation and its source; Diyanet sources come first. 50:50, Skip and Extra time are each there once per game.

03 · Daily challenge

10 questions every day.

Everyone gets the same questions, and no lives are used. Play on consecutive days to grow your streak; miss a day and it resets.

04 · Quick game

10 mixed questions from the cities you have opened.

The map doesn't advance, but your record is kept. It starts with a single tap, for a short break.

05 · Ranks and badges

From Wayfarer to World Voyager.

Your rank rises with the XP you earn: Wayfarer, Traveler, Voyager, Caravaneer, Pathfinder, Guide, Caravan Master, World Voyager. 14 badges and a statistics screen show your journey.

Privacy

No account, and your progress stays on your phone.

No account

No sign-up needed. Your progress is stored on your phone, and no data is sent to us.

Contains ads

Free, with ads and in-app purchases. Full-screen ads never appear during a game.

Questions work offline

You can play the questions without internet. Ads and purchases need a connection.
